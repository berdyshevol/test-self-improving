Approaches that caused problems and should be avoided. Maintained by the Learning Agent.

> Format:

- **Fabricating missing code/artifacts to satisfy an inapplicable task** — when a task
  references something that does not exist, inventing it just so the task "succeeds" produces
  misleading history and violates the never-invent rule. Instead, report the task blocked and
